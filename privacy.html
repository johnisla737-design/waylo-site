Waylo

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Waylo is a walking app that helps you find parks, trails and quiet green places, record your walks and enjoy nature. This policy explains what information Waylo uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The short version:

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling. Waylo has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and never sells or rents your information.
  • Your location is used only for features you use, and is usually rounded before it leaves your phone.
  • Health data stays on your phone. It is never sent to our servers.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

1. Who we are

Waylo is made by the Waylo team ("we", "us"). Contact: teamwaylosupport@gmail.com.

2. Information stored in your account

When you create an account we store:

  • Email and password (the password is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider).
  • An age range (13–15 or 16 and over), worked out from the birth year you enter at sign-up. The birth year itself is never saved or sent.
  • Your settings, daily goal and privacy choices.
  • Walks you save: date, duration, distance and steps. Walk routes are saved only if you turn that on in Profile, and are deleted after 90 days.
  • Things you create: Nature Passport stamps (place names only), your private Nature Journal (photos are stored privately, with location details removed on your phone before upload), My Walking World squares (about 500 ft squares, never the route line), favourite places, saved treasure hunts, badges and mindful sessions.
  • Community features you choose to use: your public handle, friends, group memberships and what you post in groups, reviews, trail reports and nature sounds you share, quiet-place reports (kept 60 days), sharing choices, people you block, and campaigns you join.
  • Reports you send about content or people, kept for moderators for up to 180 days.

3. Location

Waylo uses your location only while you use features that need it: showing where you are, recording a walk you start, finding nature, weather and food near you, and directions. If you turn on "Keep recording when the screen is off", Waylo also uses your location while a walk is recording with the screen locked; it stops when you pause or finish the walk.

When a feature asks an outside service for information, Waylo usually sends a rounded position (for example about 330 ft for nearby places, about 0.6 miles for weather), not your exact location. Directions are the exception: your exact start point and destination go to Mapbox to find a walking route.

4. Health and motion data

If you allow it, Waylo reads steps (and heart rate, only if you turn on heart-rate guidance in the walking coach) from Apple Health, and can save finished walks to Apple Health as walking workouts. Health data stays on your phone. It is never sent to our servers, never shared, and never used for advertising.

5. Camera, photos and microphone

The camera and photo library are used only when you add a photo to your Nature Journal or a walking group album. Location and time details are removed from photos on your phone before upload. The microphone is used only while you record a nature sound after tapping Record; nothing is recorded in the background.

6. Buddy (AI walking companion)

Messages you send to Buddy go through our server to OpenAI to write a reply. We do not store them; OpenAI may keep them for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. Your location is never sent to Buddy. Walking stats are included only if you allow it both in Buddy and in Profile.

7. Services that receive information

Waylo uses these services to work. Each receives only what is needed for the feature you are using:

  • Supabase (our database and sign-in provider): your account and the information in section 2.
  • Mapbox: maps, address search, directions, place names for passport stamps and satellite views of places.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass servers): rounded positions or areas, to find parks, trails, food, benches and landmarks.
  • Wikimedia (Wikipedia, Wikidata, Commons): place names, ids and the positions of public places shown, to find photos, logos and descriptions. Never your position.
  • Open-Meteo: rounded positions for weather, sun and elevation.
  • iNaturalist: your area rounded to about 0.6 miles and species searches, to show plants and wildlife.
  • OpenAI: Buddy messages, and the text of community posts for automatic moderation (without your name or location).
  • U.S. National Park Service: through our server, park names for official alerts.

The in-app Settings > Privacy screen lists exactly what each feature sends and how much it is rounded.

8. What other people can see

Other walkers see only what you choose to share: your public handle on posts, and, if you turn it on, weekly walking totals for friends or group challenges. They never see your routes, start points or location. Friends are added only with a single-use code you send yourself.

9. Stored only on your phone

Some information stays on your device: recent searches, recent Discover results (so the app opens quickly), comfort preferences, collection favourites, and health data. Recent searches and Discover results are removed when you sign out.

10. How long we keep information

We keep your account information until you delete your account. Walk routes are deleted after 90 days, quiet-place reports after 60 days, and reports to moderators after up to 180 days.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. This removes all information in your account.
  • Turn route saving, sharing and other features on or off at any time in Profile and Settings.
  • Turn off location, health, camera, photo or microphone access in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your information. Contact us at teamwaylosupport@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

12. Children

Waylo is for people aged 13 and over; you must be at least 13 to create an account. Family missions store no information about children. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and your sign-in is kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and collect as little as we can.

14. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
