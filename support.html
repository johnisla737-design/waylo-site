Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email teamwaylosupport@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 days.

No parks or places show up

Waylo needs your location to look nearby. Check iPhone Settings > Waylo > Location is set to "While Using the App". Place data comes from the free OpenStreetMap community servers, which are sometimes busy; if you see "Try again", wait a moment and tap it.

My walk stopped recording when the screen turned off

Turn on "Keep recording when the screen is off" for the walk, and allow location "Always" when your iPhone asks. Waylo uses it only while a walk is recording.

Steps or heart rate are missing

Open iPhone Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Waylo and turn on the data you want Waylo to read. Health data stays on your phone.

Distances say "estimate"

Distances to places are straight lines to the middle of each place, and walking times are estimates. Waylo labels anything it can't measure exactly so you know what to trust.

Delete my account

In the app, go to Settings > Account > Delete account and enter your password. This permanently removes your account and everything saved in it. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

Report a post or a person

Use the Report option on the post, group photo or friend. Our moderators review reports. For anything urgent, email us.
